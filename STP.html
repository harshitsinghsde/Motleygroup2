Multi-Stage Treatment Process

Combining primary physical separation, biological aeration, biological sludge management, and tertiary disinfection.

01

Raw Sewage Inlet & Screening

Initial mechanical bar screens remove heavy debris, grit, and large suspended solids to protect downstream pumping and processing equipment.

02

Primary Clarification

Sedimentation tanks settle organic solids and remove oil/grease, drastically reducing initial suspended solids and organic load.

03

Aeration & Biological Digestion

High-efficiency surface/fine bubble aeration tanks foster aerobic micro-organisms to rapidly degrade organic nutrients and reduce BOD.

04

Secondary Clarification

Secondary clarifiers separate treated clean water from bio-sludge mass, which is directed into sludge thickeners and drying beds.

05

Tertiary Sand & Carbon Filtration

Pressure sand filters and activated carbon filters trap microscopic particulate matter and eliminate residual odors and trace organics.

06

UV & Chlorine Disinfection

Final polishing through ultraviolet disinfection systems and controlled chlorine contact tanks ensures pathogen-free treated effluent outlet.
